About the Ranch

Heritage Harvest Ranch is a family operation in Basin, Wyoming. We grow pivot-irrigated alfalfa on ground covered by Wyoming senior water rights — the same water that’s been feeding the Big Horn Basin for generations.

We sell direct, no middlemen. If you have a question about what we have, when it’s ready, or what it costs, you get a straight answer from the people who grew it.
