Heritage Harvest Ranch is located in Basin, Wyoming &mdash; Big Horn County, in the heart of the Big Horn Basin. It&rsquo;s flat, wide-open country with a long history of agriculture built on serious water rights and serious people.

## What We Grow

Our focus right now is alfalfa hay. We run a pivot-irrigated stand established in 2024, irrigated by a 7-tower center pivot drawing off Wyoming senior water rights. Senior water rights in Wyoming mean we&rsquo;re among the first to receive water and among the last to face curtailment when supplies tighten &mdash; which matters in a dry year.

We produce **4&times;6 net-wrapped round bales** (approximately 1,100 lbs) and **small square bales** (approximately 65 lbs). Round bales suit commercial cattle operations and larger horse facilities. Small squares are the right fit for buyers who need easy handling and daily feeding.

Forage analysis is available on request. Ask when you inquire.

## How We Sell

Direct from the ranch, no middlemen. If you want to know what we have, when it&rsquo;s coming off, and what it costs, you call or email and you get a real answer.

Pickup is at the ranch in Basin. For larger orders within Big Horn County, delivery may be possible &mdash; ask when you reach out. Pricing varies with the season, the cutting, and the market, so we don&rsquo;t post fixed prices online. When you contact us, you&rsquo;ll get a current number.

## Why Buy Direct

We&rsquo;re a small operation. We know what&rsquo;s on the field, when it was cut, what the moisture was, and what the forage test showed. Buying direct means you get that information from the person who grew it.

That matters if you&rsquo;re managing horses, dairy cattle, or any livestock where forage quality drives performance. It also means there&rsquo;s no broker margin built into the price.

## Water and Land

The Big Horn Basin has some of Wyoming&rsquo;s most productive irrigated agriculture, and the senior water rights attached to this ground are part of that history. In a state where water is the limiting factor, senior rights are worth understanding when you&rsquo;re evaluating a hay supplier&rsquo;s reliability year over year.

We track stand quality and forage test results. We&rsquo;ll share them with you.
