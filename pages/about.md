Heritage Harvest Ranch is located in Basin, Wyoming &mdash; Big Horn County, in the heart of the Big Horn Basin. It&rsquo;s flat, wide-open country with a long history of agriculture built on serious water rights and serious people.

## What We Grow

Our focus right now is alfalfa hay. We run a pivot-irrigated stand established in 2024, irrigated by a 7-tower center pivot.

We produce **5&times;6 net-wrapped round bales** (approximately 1,100 lbs) perfect for cattle operations and larger horse facilities.

## How We Sell

Direct from the ranch, no middlemen. If you want to know what we have, when it&rsquo;s coming off, and what it costs, feel free to call or email.

Pickup is at the ranch in Basin. For larger orders within Big Horn County, delivery may be possible &mdash; ask when you reach out. Pricing varies with the season, the cutting, and the market, so we don&rsquo;t post fixed prices online.

## Why Buy Direct

We&rsquo;re a small operation. We know what&rsquo;s on the field, when it was cut, what the moisture was, and what the forage test showed.
