*Last updated: May 2026*

Heritage Harvest Ranch operates this website at heritageharvestranch.github.io. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

## Information We Collect

### Analytics (Google Analytics 4)

We use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to understand how visitors use this site. GA4 automatically collects:

- Pages visited and time spent on each page
- General geographic location (country/region level)
- Device type, browser, and operating system
- How you arrived at the site (search, direct, referral)

GA4 sets cookies in your browser to track sessions and distinguish visitors. These cookies do not identify you personally.

You can opt out of GA4 tracking by installing the [Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

### Contact and Order Inquiry Forms

When you submit a contact or order inquiry form on this site, we collect:

- Your name and email address
- Your phone number (optional)
- Any message or notes you provide
- Product context (which product you are inquiring about)

Form submissions are processed by [Formspree](https://formspree.io) and delivered by email to Heritage Harvest Ranch. Formspree's privacy policy governs how they handle form data in transit. We retain the resulting emails according to normal business email practices.

### Information You Do Not Need to Provide

We do not require you to create an account, and we do not collect payment information through this site.

## How We Use Your Information

- **Analytics data** is used solely to understand site traffic and improve content.
- **Form submission data** is used to respond to your inquiry or order request.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

## Cookies

This site uses cookies set by Google Analytics. No other cookies are set by this site.

Most browsers allow you to control or block cookies through browser settings. Blocking analytics cookies will not affect your ability to use this site.

## Data Retention

We do not control the retention of analytics data collected by Google. Form submission data is retained in our email as long as it is relevant to ongoing business operations.

## Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you. To exercise these rights, contact us at [info@heritageharvestranch.com](mailto:info@heritageharvestranch.com).

## Children's Privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
